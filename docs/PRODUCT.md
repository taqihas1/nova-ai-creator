# Nova AI Creator — Product Definition

## Positioning

Nova AI Creator is the platform where users hire an AI content creation executive team to run project-based campaigns.

## Initial customer

- Content creators
- Small businesses
- Founders
- Marketing consultants
- Agencies
- Bloggers and video creators

## Core product promise

Create a project, give the executive team an outcome, and receive coordinated deliverables for LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok.

## Product principles

1. Everything belongs to a Project.
2. Users assign outcomes, not isolated prompts.
3. Executives own responsibilities and collaborate.
4. Nova may use multiple LLMs through a provider-neutral Model Gateway.
5. Auto routing is the default; manual provider control is available for advanced users.
6. Content creation remains the first vertical until the experience is excellent.

## Content Creation Executive Team

- Chief Content Officer
- Research Director
- Content Strategist
- Copy Director
- Social Media Director
- Video Director
- Design Director
- Publishing Manager
- Analytics Director

## Future expansion

The same executive-team architecture can later support focused vertical packs such as marketing, legal, finance and investment banking without adding those domains to the core content product today.
